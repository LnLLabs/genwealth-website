# Clarify GenWealth’s message and update its logo

## Goal
Keep the current visual identity and colors, replace the existing logo with the supplied transparent logo, and rewrite the full site so every visitor quickly understands what GenWealth does, why it matters, and what they gain.

## Messaging approach
Apply Donald Miller’s StoryBrand principles without copying book text:

- Make the visitor the central character and GenWealth the guide.
- Lead with the concrete problem: wealth can be legally assigned but still difficult to find, validate, and transfer.
- Explain GenWealth in plain language before introducing terms such as “infrastructure” or “programmable inheritance.”
- Give each audience a clear desired outcome, problem, short plan, benefit, and next action.
- Show the stakes honestly: less searching, delay, manual coordination, and uncertainty—without exaggerated claims.
- Use one consistent explanation across pages: GenWealth connects the owner’s instructions, the legal professional’s validation, and the institution holding the asset so inheritance can be carried out.

## Page changes

### Homepage
- Replace the abstract opening with a plain-English headline and one-sentence explanation.
- Present a simple three-step plan: prepare, validate, transfer.
- Make the benefits for lawyers/notaries, institutions, and individuals immediately scannable.
- Keep the interactive wealth-transfer visual, but simplify its labels and supporting copy.
- Reduce repetition and use one primary action per audience.

### Audience pages
- **Lawyers & notaries:** emphasize a better client service, less repetitive administration, and a practical way to handle digital assets while preserving their legal role.
- **Institutions:** emphasize lower operational burden, clearer workflows, better customer experience, and integration with existing systems.
- **Individuals:** emphasize control today, clarity for loved ones, and preparation with a legal professional.

### Supporting pages
- **How it works:** condense the process into clear stages and distinguish who does what.
- **Technology:** translate technical capabilities into outcomes while retaining enough detail for institutional readers.
- **About:** state the vision plainly and connect the long-term ambition to the current digital-asset starting point.
- **Contact:** tailor the invitation and expected outcome to the selected visitor type.

## Logo and presentation
- Upload the supplied transparent logo as the site’s new logo and use it in the header and footer.
- Derive the favicon from the supplied brand mark.
- Preserve the current purple, dark-background palette and overall visual system.
- Keep layouts and interactions stable except where small copy-layout changes improve clarity.

## Quality checks
- Verify every page at desktop and mobile widths.
- Check the new logo against the dark background and confirm it is not clipped or distorted.
- Confirm every page has complete, unique sharing and search metadata.
- Confirm all links, audience paths, and the interactive transfer visual still work.
